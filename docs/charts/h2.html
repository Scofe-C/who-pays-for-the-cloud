The tax break grew faster than the new tax can recoup

Virginia’s data center sales and use tax exemption: benefit reported by operators, FY2023 to FY2025, $ billions (self-reported, state + local). Toggle to compare the annual revenue cap of the new 1.1¢/kWh tax.

JLARC: about 48¢ of state revenue comes back for every $1 of exemption.

FY2023 is from JLARC (state, local and regional sales tax); later years are from the Virginia Department of Taxation’s RD40 report. All figures are self-reported by operators.

New tax: 1.1¢ per kWh from July 1, 2026, revenue capped at $0.6B a year (FY2027 onward); amounts above the cap are refunded. Secondary source (law-firm summary of the budget bill, HB 30).

Sources: JLARC Report 598, Data Centers in Virginia; Virginia Dept. of Taxation, Biennial Data Center Retail Sales and Use Tax Exemption Report (RD40, Jan 2, 2026); Williams Mullen, Virginia budget creates new electricity consumption tax for data centers (secondary; HB 30). Data snapshot 2026-11-07.
