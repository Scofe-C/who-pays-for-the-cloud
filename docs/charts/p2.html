Data-center construction now outspends general office buildings

Private construction spending, monthly, January 2018 to September 2026. Data centers have been above general office every month since September 2025.

Nominal dollars. Construction spending covers buildings and their systems; servers and racks are not included.

General office = the Census “General” office subcategory (the Census “Office” total includes data centers). Recent months are preliminary or revised; September 2026 is preliminary.

The lines first crossed in April 2025, then traded places until September 2025.

Source: U.S. Census Bureau, Construction Spending (C30), private SAAR; November 2, 2026 release.
