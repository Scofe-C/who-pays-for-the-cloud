Growth alone didn’t raise prices

Of the 29 states whose electricity sales grew from 2019 to 2024, 25 saw their average power price fall after inflation. Each dot is a state or D.C.; right = sold more, down = cheaper.

● whole state in PJM  ○ part of state in PJM  ● not in PJM

All sectors, annual averages. Real price = nominal average retail price deflated with CPI-U (annual mean of 12 months) to 2024 dollars.

PJM status: membership per pjm.com; the whole-state vs part-of-state split is our classification.

Source: U.S. EIA, electricity retail sales (API v2); BLS CPI-U (CUUR0000SA0). Data snapshot 2026-11-07.
